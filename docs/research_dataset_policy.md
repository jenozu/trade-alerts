# Research dataset freeze and holdout policy

**2023–2025 = DEVELOPMENT / RESEARCH DATA.** Repeated inspection and candidate
selection have consumed these periods as independent validation. They may develop
hypotheses; they cannot independently prove rules selected using the same sample.
The machine-readable authority is config/research_policy.yaml. Archived reports
retain their original wording/results as historical evidence; this policy governs
all new interpretation and metadata.

2026 is pseudo-out-of-sample / validation, not a proven untouched holdout. Manual
NQ analysis has informed strategy development. No specific untouched 2026 portion
is certified. Unknown/future years are unclassified, never automatically holdouts.

The integrity checkpoint configuration hashes in docs/integrity_checkpoint.json
freeze the identity of the configuration examined here, **not an approved forward
strategy model**. It must not be called Model Freeze v1. Fidelity issues and input
provenance must be resolved before that freeze can be declared.

Before examining forward outcomes, preregister a model/config/code/input lock and
an unexamined future period with exact ET start/end, entry/exit/session policies,
costs, eligible families, expected metrics, exclusions and review date. October
2026 is only an example, not an automatically untouched sample. Use fingerprints
for every run; retain old model/cache identities.

During the forward period there are no outcome-driven weights/thresholds/features,
setup definitions, or retroactive exclusions. A correctness bug fix requires a
written reason, regression test and new version; retain the original version's
results and decide prospectively whether a new forward period is necessary.
Repeated review/tuning consumes a sample into development data. A favorable result
alone does not certify an edge. Strategy selection remains paused at this gate.
