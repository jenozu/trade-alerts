# F003 — 90–100 sample is too small

Status: Preliminary

Source: [[../Experiments/EXP-001-Score-Band-Analysis]]

## Finding

The 90–100 score band produced the strongest headline performance in EXP-001, but the sample is too small to trust for calibration decisions.

## Evidence

- 21 trades
- 38.1% win rate
- +13.70 pts expectancy
- +0.548R expectancy
- PF 1.88
- +287.75 net points

## Limitation

Do not raise weights, create a 90+ production threshold, or treat this band as validated until substantially more out-of-sample evidence exists.
