# F002 — 80–89 outperforms 70–79

Status: Supported

Source: [[../Experiments/EXP-001-Score-Band-Analysis]]

## Finding

The 80–89 score band materially outperformed 70–79 on expectancy and profit factor despite a very similar win rate.

## Evidence

- 70–79: 958 trades, 27.7% win rate, +0.82 pts expectancy, PF 1.05, +788.00 net points.
- 80–89: 239 trades, 27.2% win rate, +4.59 pts expectancy, PF 1.25, +1096.75 net points.

## Interpretation

The score appears to identify better payoff/follow-through quality rather than simply increasing win probability.
