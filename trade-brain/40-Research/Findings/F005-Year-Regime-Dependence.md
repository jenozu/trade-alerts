# F005 — Year/regime dependence

Status: Preliminary

Source: [[../Experiments/EXP-001-Score-Band-Analysis]]

## Finding

Score-band behavior is not stable enough across individual years to assume a single universal relationship between score and outcome.

## Evidence

EXP-001 showed materially different year-by-year behavior within the same score bands. In particular, 70–79 was strongest in 2023 and much weaker in later years, while 80–89 improved materially in 2024–2025.

## Implication

Future scorer calibration must be evaluated across years/regimes and then validated out-of-sample rather than optimized on aggregate results alone.
