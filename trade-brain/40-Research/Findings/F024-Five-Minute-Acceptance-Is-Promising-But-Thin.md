# F024 — Five-minute acceptance is promising but sample-limited

Status: Preliminary

Sources:
- [[../Experiments/EXP-008-Breakout-Acceptance-Quality]]

## Finding

Existing continuation trades that already had a completed five-minute close beyond the broken structure level before signal time materially outperformed other continuations.

## Evidence

Overall:

- without completed 5m acceptance: 163 trades, +4.68 pts expectancy, PF 1.27;
- with completed 5m acceptance: 22 trades, +15.17 pts, PF 1.94.

By year:

- 2023: +12.50 / PF 1.87, n=7;
- 2024: +16.42 / PF 1.98, n=6;
- 2025: +16.42 / PF 1.98, n=9.

## Interpretation

The sign is encouraging and cross-year consistent, but only 22 historical trades qualify.

The evidence supports a later explicit confirmation-entry experiment, not a production 5m-close requirement.
