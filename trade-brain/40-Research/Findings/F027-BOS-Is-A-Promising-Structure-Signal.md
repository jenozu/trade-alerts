# F027 — Same-direction BOS is a promising structure signal

Status: Preliminary

Sources:
- [[../Experiments/EXP-009-Displacement]]

## Finding

Trades with same-direction BOS at signal time materially outperformed trades without it.

## Evidence

- BOS absent: 1,135 trades, +1.07 pts expectancy / PF 1.06.
- BOS present: 83 trades, +11.57 pts / PF 1.74.

## Interpretation

The effect is large enough to justify dedicated structure analysis.

However, BOS may overlap with displacement, continuation classification, FVG creation, or other structure features.

EXP-010 must test its incremental value and redundancy before any scoring change.
