# F014 — 2024 weakness was lower favorable excursion, not higher adverse excursion

Status: Supported

Source: [[../Experiments/EXP-004-Year-and-Regime-Stability]]

## Finding

The nearly breakeven 2024 baseline showed weaker favorable movement and lower target reach than the mean of 2023 and 2025, without a corresponding increase in adverse excursion.

## Evidence

2024 versus the mean of 2023 and 2025:

- Average MFE: 36.95 vs 40.51 points.
- Median MFE: 20.50 vs 25.06 points.
- Average MAE: 25.75 vs 27.21 points.
- Median MAE: 27.00 vs 27.81 points.
- TP1: 44.3% vs 48.9%.
- TP2: 30.9% vs 33.8%.
- TP3: 20.9% vs 22.1%.
- TP4: 12.6% vs 15.0%.
- Stop rate: 70.6% vs 69.8%.

## Interpretation

2024 weakness looks more like reduced opportunity/extension after entry than unusually greater adverse movement. The average trade also remained open longer than the peer-year mean, consistent with weaker follow-through.

## Implication

Later exit, target-capture, regime, and setup-quality research should distinguish insufficient extension from excessive initial risk. EXP-004 does not justify changing stops or targets by itself.
