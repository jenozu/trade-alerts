# EXP-002 — Long vs Short

Status: Implementation complete; baseline ledger execution pending

## Question

Does the current strategy exhibit meaningful and stable directional asymmetry between LONG and SHORT trades, and where does that asymmetry come from?

## Control

Untouched completed annual baselines for 2023, 2024, and 2025. EXP-002 is diagnostic only.

No score weights, entry logic, stop logic, exit logic, setup qualification, or long/short configuration split is changed by this experiment.

## Inputs

- 2023 ledger: `/docker/trade-alerts-2023/data/results/backtest/trades.csv`
- 2024 ledger: `/docker/trade-alerts-2024/data/results/backtest/trades.csv`
- 2025 ledger: `/docker/trade-alerts/data/results/research_runs/2025_pipeline_final/backtest/trades.csv`

The 20-stage pipelines must not be rerun for EXP-002.

## Implementation

- Analyzer: `src/directional_research.py`
- Runner: `scripts/run_exp002_long_vs_short.py`
- Tests: `tests/test_directional_research.py`
- Default outputs: `data/reports/EXP-002_long-vs-short/`

Artifacts generated by the runner:

- `EXP-002_long-vs-short.md`
- `exp002_long_vs_short_results.json`
- `directional_metrics.csv`

## Required comparisons

The analyzer compares LONG vs SHORT on:

- trade count
- win rate
- expectancy points
- expectancy R when present
- profit factor
- net points
- TP1 / TP2 / TP3 / TP4 hit rate
- stop rate
- average and median MFE
- average and median MAE
- average hold time when supported
- average / median score and score-band distribution
- score-band performance
- year-by-year performance
- time-of-day buckets when an entry timestamp is available
- HTF bias and derived HTF alignment when available
- DOL direction when available
- liquidity sweep presence/direction representation when available
- displacement when available
- structure shift when available
- FVG context when available
- SNR and RVOL directional distributions and quartile performance when available

## Sample-size discipline

Direction-level conclusions must be checked for stability across 2023, 2024, and 2025. Context cells below 30 trades are exploratory and must not be treated as validated evidence.

## Prior evidence from EXP-001

[[EXP-001-Score-Band-Analysis]] exposed an initial directional asymmetry:

- 70–79: long +1.67 pts expectancy / PF 1.10 versus short -0.14 / PF 0.99.
- 80–89: long +7.52 pts expectancy / PF 1.43 versus short +1.04 / PF 1.05.

EXP-002 is intended to determine whether this is stable and what contexts explain it.
